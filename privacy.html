Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Summary

doodlz.app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. We do not build player profiles and we do not sell data. The only third party that processes data on this site is Google, for advertising and traffic measurement.

2. What is stored on your device

Your chosen nickname and avatar are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device, so that you do not have to set them up again next time. You can clear them at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

3. What the game server sees

While you are in a room, the server holds your nickname, your avatar, your score, your chat messages and your drawing strokes in memory so it can relay them to the other players in that room. When the room closes, all of it is discarded. Nothing about a finished game is written to a database.

Standard technical data such as your IP address and browser user agent is processed transiently to run the realtime connection, to prevent abuse and to enforce short kick cooldowns.

4. Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • More detail is available in Google's advertising policies and Google's privacy policy.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California and similar regions), a consent or opt-out choice is presented through Google's certified consent management message before personalised ads are shown. If you decline, non-personalised ads are served instead. Ads are never placed on top of the game canvas or the chat.

5. Children

doodlz.app is intended to be suitable for a general audience, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Personalised advertising is not directed at users identified as children. If you believe a child has typed personal information into the chat, email us and we will remove it.

6. Your choices

You can clear local storage, block cookies, use your browser's private mode, or adjust ad personalisation through the links above. To request removal of content or data, email wizards72727@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the current version, and continued use after a change means you accept the updated version.
