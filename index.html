doodlz.app is a free online multiplayer drawing and guessing game. Each turn one player draws a secret word while everybody else races to guess it in the chat. The faster you guess, the more points you score. No download and no account required — just press Play.

No cheating: while a round is running, the guessers cannot see the current drawer's chat messages, so the drawer can never spell out or hint at the word.

  1. Pick one of the words you are offered to draw.
  2. Draw it — no letters, numbers or spelling allowed.
  3. Let the other players guess your drawing in the chat.
  4. On your turn to watch, guess the other drawings as fast as you can.
  5. The player with the most points at the end wins.
